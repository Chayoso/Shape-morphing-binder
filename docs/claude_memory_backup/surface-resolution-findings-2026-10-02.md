Chain of diagnostics on 2026-10-02 (all in docs/experiments.md, probes in scripts/probes/settled/):

- **D12** (`render_axes.py`): the 4K blur is mostly the SAMPLE. 300k volume particles = one surface particle per 13.8 px; ~90 % of particles are interior; dragon teeth/scales are below a lattice step and absent from the target sample. Normal error vs the mesh 18.5° (300k) → 13.3° (1.2M) → 10.9° (2.4M); renderer-width changes gain ≤ 20 % on the target and worsen morph frames.
- **D13** (`surface_tracers.py`): the SOURCE surface carried through a whole run as massless tracers fails (area ×3.7–4.6, 5–6 % folds, 18–25 % of the area over gaps, 10 % of the final surface uncovered): interior material surfaces. Never propose whole-run source-surface tracers again.
- **Agent assessment of Chen et al. 2021 resampling** (arXiv 2101.12408): their surface = union of particle spheres of radius 0.867 Δx + marching cubes, samples live one step; a re-extracted surface holds no more than the particles; G2P-only tracers miss the u channel and the relaxation; a per-window sample set breaks "merit = function of the committed state". Verdict: does not address the measured limits.
- **D14** (`subcell_response.py`, slab at rest, open loop): the rollout holds and the u channel makes relief down to the particle sampling limit; the stress channel (dFc) works down to about one cell; the OUTER-LAYER RELAXATION (`k_layer_project`, 24 layer neighbours, 2 spacings, 1/T per step on all 40 steps) removes relief of ≤ 6 spacings within 1–2 windows and half of 11-spacing relief in 4 windows; ≥ 22 spacings is kept. With the relaxation off everything holds (control arm). The limit is in SPACINGS, not cells: at 300k 11 sp = 2 cells = 0.6 wu.
- Corrections to remember: transfers are CUBIC B-splines (4³ stencil), not quadratic. The server clock, not my estimate, stamps a pre-registration (again two minutes ahead once today).
