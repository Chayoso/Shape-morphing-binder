**Requirement (user, 2026-10-04 ~16:00 CDT, verbatim):** "ㄴㄴ 300K + 에서 무조건 'render gradient'를 먹인 게 'physics only'보다 좋아야 해. 모든 metric과 momentum 보존까지 전부. A, B 둘다 봐야 할 거 같네."

Said in reply to my offering "300k physics-only, render only at 40k" as an option after D70–D73 showed the render arm behind its physics-only twin on the 300k dragon.

**Why:** the project's premise is that rendering controls physics ([[always-report-render-influence]], [[competitor-landscape]]); a result where the render term does not help at the paper's resolution defeats it.

**How to apply:**
- Never propose dropping the render term at high N. When the render arm is behind, the task is to find which definition makes it behind and fix that ([[no-parameter-only-fixes]]).
- Acceptance for any render-path change at 300k+: with-render ≥ physics-only twin on all of: display IoU and picture difference (front, thin crop, far side), yardstick render terms, offset bands and normals, arrangement, thin uncovered, chamfer, holes, transport energy, residual kinetic energy at the end, and momentum conservation (centre-of-mass drift, angular momentum) — all kept frames, both bunny and dragon ([[exterior-layer-stages-2-3-directive]]).
- A = the render weight (λ set once, share grows to 0.94 and starves the physics objective), B = the render picture's resolution against the particle pitch (64 px = 3.8 pitches at 300k): both to be examined, one change per experiment ([[min-spacing-d70-d72]] has D73's evidence).
